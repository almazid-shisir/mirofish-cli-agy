# Announcement: City AI Traffic Management Policy

The City Transportation Department announced today that starting next month, a fully autonomous AI-driven dynamic pricing and lane allocation system will be deployed across all downtown bridges and expressways.

Key Points:
1. Dynamic Congestion Tolls: Peak-hour tolls will automatically adjust every 15 minutes between $2.00 and $15.00 based on real-time traffic volume.
2. AI Priority Lanes: High-occupancy vehicles (3+ passengers) and clean-energy buses get prioritized green lights and free access.
3. Privacy & Surveillance Concerns: Over 200 automated license plate and facial-scanning cameras are installed at all entry points.
4. Public Debate: Commuters express frustration over unpredictable toll costs, while transit advocacy groups applaud reduced commute times and reduced emissions. Local delivery companies fear increased operating costs.
